What is a quotation?

A quotation (or price quote) is a document you send to a potential client before work begins, stating the price you're proposing for specific products or services. It gives the client a clear figure to approve, decline, or negotiate — and it's not a bill, since no work has started yet.

Quotation vs. invoice: what's the difference?

A quotation is sent before work starts, to agree on price. An invoice is sent after (or during) the work, to request payment. Once a client accepts your quotation and the job is done, switch to the invoice generator to bill them.

How to create a quotation

  1. Add your business details and logo.
  2. Add the client's name and contact details.
  3. List the proposed products or services with quantity and price.
  4. Set how long the quote is valid — clients need a deadline to decide by.
  5. Add terms (e.g. deposit required, payment schedule) and download or print.

What should a quotation include?

  • A quotation number and issue date, separate from any future invoice number
  • A clear expiry date — prices can change, so state how long the quote stands
  • An itemized breakdown so the client understands what they're paying for
  • Terms such as deposit requirements, payment schedule, or what's excluded

Frequently asked questions

Is the quotation generator free?

Yes — creating, previewing, printing and downloading quotations is free, with no signup required.

Can I turn a quotation into an invoice later?

Once a client accepts your quote, head to the invoice generator and re-enter the agreed items to bill for the work.

How long should a quotation be valid?

That depends on your industry and how quickly your costs change — 14 or 30 days is common. You can set this in the "Valid For" field.
