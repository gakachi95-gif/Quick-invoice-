What is QuickInvoice?

QuickInvoice is a free, browser-based tool for creating professional business documents — invoices, receipts, quotations and estimates — without signing up for an account or learning accounting software. Fill in your details, watch your document update live, then download it as a PDF or print it.

Who QuickInvoice is for

Freelancers, contractors, consultants, small business owners and agencies who need to send a professional-looking document quickly. If you only bill clients occasionally, or you want something faster than a full invoicing platform, QuickInvoice gets you a finished PDF in a few minutes.

Why QuickInvoice is different

  • No account needed. Start creating a document immediately — nothing to sign up for.
  • Your data stays with you. Everything, including your logo and signature, is stored only in your browser and never uploaded to a server.
  • Four document types, one place. Invoices, receipts, quotations and estimates all share the same fast, mobile-friendly editor.
  • Built for mobile. Create and download a document from your phone just as easily as from a desktop.

Get started

Ready to create your first document? Create a free invoice, try the receipt generator, or browse invoice templates to see the available styles first.
