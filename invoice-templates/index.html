How invoice templates work in QuickInvoice

Every template uses the same invoice generator underneath — your business details, items, taxes and totals stay the same, only the visual layout and accent color change. That means you can preview your actual invoice in each style rather than a generic mockup, and switch between templates at any point while editing without losing your data.

Choosing the right template

  • Classic — a dependable, traditional look that suits most service businesses.
  • Modern — a colored header band that works well for creative and design-led brands.
  • Minimal — a quiet, precise layout for freelancers and consultants.
  • Professional — a structured, side-bar layout for contractors and B2B invoicing.

Simple invoice and freelancer invoice layouts

If you're looking for a "simple invoice" or "freelancer invoice" specifically, the Minimal and Classic templates work well as a starting point — pick either one, then use the sidebar in the invoice generator to set your own accent color.

Printable invoices

Every template is print-ready. Once you're happy with your invoice, use Print Invoice for a clean paper copy, or Download PDF to save and email it.
