# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 15, 2026**

Welcome to **QuickInvoice** ("QuickInvoice", "we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the QuickInvoice website and invoice generator.

## 1. About QuickInvoice

QuickInvoice is a free online invoice generator that allows users to create professional invoices, calculate totals, download invoices as PDF files, print invoices, and use other invoice-related features directly through their web browser.

QuickInvoice is designed to work without requiring users to create an account.

## 2. Invoice Information

QuickInvoice may allow you to enter information including:

- Business name
- Business email
- Business phone number
- Business address
- Business website
- Customer name
- Customer email
- Customer phone number
- Customer address
- Invoice number
- Invoice date
- Due date
- Products or services
- Quantities and prices
- Tax and discount information
- Notes and payment terms
- Business logos
- Signatures
- Other images added to an invoice

### Local Processing

QuickInvoice is designed to process invoice information primarily within your web browser.

Unless a feature specifically states otherwise, QuickInvoice does not intentionally upload your invoice information to our servers for storage.

Some information may be stored locally in your browser to allow the application to function properly.

Because this information may remain on your device, you should protect access to your device and browser.

## 3. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit QuickInvoice, certain technical information may be processed automatically by QuickInvoice or third-party services.

This may include:

- IP address

- Operating system
- Approximate location
- Pages visited
- Features used
- Referring website
- General usage information
- Advertising-related information

This information may be used for website functionality, security, analytics, advertising, and improving QuickInvoice.
