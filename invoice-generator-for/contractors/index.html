Invoice Generator for Contractors

Contractor billing usually means splitting labor from materials, and sometimes invoicing in stages as a project progresses. QuickInvoice handles all of that with simple line items — no specialized construction software required.

Create a Free Invoice

Why contractors use QuickInvoice

  • Labor and materials as separate line items — keep costs transparent so clients see exactly what they're paying for.
  • Deposits and progress billing — use the Amount Paid field to track partial payments and show a running Balance Due as a job progresses.
  • Photos on the invoice — attach a site photo or completed-work photo directly to the document as supporting evidence.
  • Signatures — capture a client sign-off directly on the invoice, drawn or uploaded.
  • Works from a job site — the mobile-friendly editor means you can send an invoice from your phone before you've even left the property.

What to put on a contractor invoice

Separating labor from materials avoids disputes and makes it easy for clients to see where the cost comes from.

  • "Labor — framing, 3 days" — Qty 3, day rate
  • "Materials — lumber and fasteners" — Qty 1, materials total
  • "Deposit received" — tracked in the Amount Paid field, shown against the Balance Due

Tips for contractor invoicing

  • Request a deposit before starting larger jobs, and note it clearly using the Amount Paid field so the balance is obvious.
  • Attach a photo of completed work — it's useful documentation if there's ever a dispute.
  • Break multi-stage projects into progress invoices rather than one large invoice at the end.
  • Keep materials receipts separate from your invoice records so your markup, if any, stays clear internally.

Frequently asked questions

Can I show a deposit and remaining balance on one invoice?

Yes — enter the amount already paid in the Payment section, and the invoice will automatically show the remaining Balance Due.

Can I attach a photo of the finished job?

Yes, use the Photos & Attachments section on the invoice to add up to 6 images.

Can a client sign the invoice?

Yes — add a signature by drawing on-screen or uploading a signature image.
