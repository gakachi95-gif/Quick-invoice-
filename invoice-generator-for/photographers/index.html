Invoice Generator for Photographers

Photography billing often mixes a session fee with prints, digital delivery, and sometimes usage rights. QuickInvoice lets you itemize all of it clearly, so clients know exactly what their payment covers.

Create a Free Invoice

Why photographers use QuickInvoice

  • Package-based billing — list your session fee as one line item, with prints, albums or digital files as separate ones.
  • Booking deposits — take a deposit to secure a date, and let the invoice show the remaining balance due after the shoot.
  • Licensing and usage notes — use the Terms & Conditions field to note usage rights or print release terms.
  • A visual-first look — add your logo and choose a template that matches your studio's brand.
  • Works on location — send an invoice from your phone right after a shoot wraps.

What to put on a photography invoice

Clients respond well to itemized photography invoices — it shows exactly what they're paying for beyond just "photography."

  • "Engagement session — 1.5 hours, 2 locations" — Qty 1, session rate
  • "Digital gallery — 50 edited images" — Qty 1, delivery fee
  • "8x10 print" — Qty 5, price per print

Tips for photography invoicing

  • Take a deposit for bookings, especially for weddings and events, and track it against the balance due.
  • Note usage or licensing terms clearly if clients are only licensed for personal (not commercial) use.
  • Separate your session fee from print or product costs so clients can see the value of each.
  • Send the invoice promptly after delivering the gallery, while the experience is still fresh for the client.

Frequently asked questions

Can I invoice for a booking deposit separately from the final balance?

Yes — record the deposit in the Amount Paid field once received, and the invoice shows the remaining Balance Due.

Can I note image usage rights on the invoice?

Yes, use the Terms & Conditions field to describe usage or licensing terms.

Can I add my studio logo?

Yes — upload a logo image and it appears on the invoice document and PDF.
