Invoice Generator for Web Designers

Web design work is often billed in stages — a deposit, a design milestone, a launch payment — sometimes alongside an ongoing hosting or maintenance fee. QuickInvoice makes it easy to itemize each part clearly.

Create a Free Invoice

Why web designers use QuickInvoice

  • Milestone-based billing — invoice a deposit, a design-approval payment, and a final launch payment as separate line items or separate invoices.
  • Retainers and maintenance — bill recurring hosting or maintenance fees as their own line item, separate from one-off project work.
  • Scope clarity — use the Notes field to reference what a payment covers (e.g. "Milestone 2 of 3 — homepage and 4 inner pages").
  • Deposit tracking — record deposits in Amount Paid so the client always sees an accurate Balance Due.
  • Professional templates — a Modern or Minimal template fits a design-led brand better than a generic spreadsheet invoice.

What to put on a web design invoice

Milestone invoices read best when each line item references what stage of the project it covers.

  • "Project deposit — 50% upfront" — Qty 1, deposit amount
  • "Design milestone — homepage mockup approved" — Qty 1, milestone fee
  • "Monthly hosting & maintenance" — Qty 1, recurring rate

Tips for web design invoicing

  • Split larger projects into milestone invoices rather than one invoice at the end — it improves cash flow and reduces risk.
  • Keep hosting/maintenance retainers on separate invoices from one-off project work, since they're billed on a different cycle.
  • Reference the specific deliverable or milestone in your notes so clients immediately understand the charge.
  • Track deposits against the balance due so it's always clear how much of the project cost remains.

Frequently asked questions

Can I invoice a project in stages?

Yes — either create separate invoices per milestone, or list each milestone as its own line item on one invoice.

Can I bill a recurring hosting fee?

Yes, add it as a line item; for a true recurring subscription system you'd want dedicated billing software, but this works well for manual monthly invoicing.

Can I track a project deposit against the total?

Yes — enter the deposit in the Amount Paid field, and the invoice shows the remaining Balance Due.
