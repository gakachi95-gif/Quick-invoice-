Invoice Generator for Small Business

Small businesses often bill a mix of products and services, sometimes with recurring customers and local tax rules. QuickInvoice keeps that simple with editable tax and discount fields and a consistent, branded invoice every time.

Create a Free Invoice

Why small businesses use QuickInvoice

  • Products and services in one invoice — mix physical items and service line items without any setup.
  • Tax and discounts built in — apply a tax rate or a percentage/fixed discount, and the totals recalculate automatically.
  • Your own branding — add your logo and pick an accent color so every invoice looks like it came from your business.
  • Repeat customers — your business details are remembered between invoices, so re-billing a regular customer takes seconds.
  • No software to install — works in a browser on any computer or phone your team uses.

What to put on a small business invoice

Small business invoices should read clearly whether you're selling a product, a service, or both.

  • "Product — 12oz ceramic mug (branded)" — Qty 25, unit price
  • "Delivery fee" — Qty 1, flat fee
  • "Installation service" — Qty 1, service rate

Tips for small business invoicing

  • Use a consistent invoice numbering pattern so your records stay organized as volume grows.
  • Double-check your local tax rate before applying it — QuickInvoice calculates the tax you enter, but doesn't set rates for you.
  • Keep your logo and accent color consistent across invoices so customers recognize your business at a glance.
  • Save frequent customers' details somewhere separately (a spreadsheet works fine) so re-entering their info is quick.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply my local sales tax rate?

Yes — enter your tax rate as a percentage in the Tax section, and it applies to the invoice total after any discount.

Can I offer a discount to specific customers?

Yes — choose a percentage or fixed discount amount per invoice.

Is there a limit on how many invoices I can create?

No — you can create as many invoices as you like, for free.
